Good To Know

Before you come in

01

Arrive 5 minutes early

Slots run back to back. Turning up on the dot means your cut starts late.

02

10-minute grace

More than 10 minutes late and the barber may release your slot to a walk-in. No charge either way.

03

Pay at the shop

No deposit and no card needed to book. Settle up after the cut — cash or MoMo.
